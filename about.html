Why I made this site

I'm a student who fell in love with playing drums, and I want to help other total beginners feel that same excitement. I built this website to share everything I've learned — laid out simply, made fun, and totally free.

I really believe drumming is for everyone, at any age. You don't need fancy gear, years of lessons, or natural talent. You just need a little patience, a pair of sticks (or even a pillow!), and the willingness to have fun while you practice.

These lessons are written the way I wish someone had explained things to me when I started — clear, friendly, and focused on actually playing as soon as possible.

What this site is all about

  • 🎉 Making drums fun and welcoming for all ages
  • 🆓 Keeping every single lesson free
  • 🥁 Letting you play a real kit right in your browser
  • 📈 Helping you see your progress and stay motivated
