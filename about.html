About Ian

The person behind the lessons.

🥁

Hey, I'm Ian!

I'm a student who loves playing drums and wants to help other beginners get started. I built this website to share everything I've learned on my own drumming journey — laid out clearly and for free.

I believe drumming is for everyone. You don't need expensive gear, years of lessons, or natural talent to start making music. You just need patience, a pair of sticks, and a willingness to practice.

These lessons are written the way I wish someone had explained things to me when I was first starting out — straightforward, practical, and focused on actually playing.

My Goals for This Site

  • Make drumming accessible to total beginners
  • Keep every lesson free
  • Focus on fundamentals that last a lifetime
  • Grow as I grow as a drummer
