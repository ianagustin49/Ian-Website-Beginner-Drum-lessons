🥁 The Play Room

This is YOUR drum kit. Click the drums, tap them on your phone, or use your keyboard keys. Go wild — there's no wrong way to play!

💡 On a computer? Try the keyboard: Q W E for cymbals & toms, A S D for hi-hat, snare & floor tom, and SPACE for the kick drum.

Practice Your Timing

A metronome keeps a steady beat so you learn to play in time. Start slow!

Play-Along Beats

Press a button to loop a beat. Try drumming along on the kit above!
